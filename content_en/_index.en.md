## About Me

As a child, I was shy, reserved — and always the best in my class. Together, that's not a good combination when you want to belong.

There was a boy named Simon. He bullied me for years — from primary school through high school. I even went to a high school far away to escape him. He came with me. As the only one.

I remember a break in first grade when he forced me onto a tall wooden tower on the school playground — and locked me up there. The whole break. I was paralyzed with fear. Not just because of the height. But because I didn't know what awaited me below. He was long gone. But my fear held me captive. That's exactly what trauma is: It's not the situation itself that holds us captive — but what it leaves behind in us.

Later, something important happened: I stood on a wooden tower again. And this time — I just came down.

In retrospect, that was the moment when I first felt: **I can do something. I'm not helpless.** That was the beginning of everything I do today.

Today, I accompany people who are stuck in their own wooden tower. Who know that the danger is long past — and yet can't come down. Who are repeatedly triggered by what happened back then.

### My Approach

My work is not about endlessly rehashing the past. It's about breaking the connection — between what happened then and what still holds you captive today. Efficiently, solution-focused, and with a good dose of humanity. Sometimes even with a smile.

Today I am a psychotherapy practitioner, specialized in trauma, anxiety, and depression. I know from personal experience what it feels like to be stuck. And I know just as well what it feels like to be free again.

**If you're ready — let's find your way down from the tower to freedom together.**

### My Methods

I work with highly effective, solution-focused methods that have proven themselves. Each method brings different strengths:

- **EMI/EMDR**: Uses eye movements to process traumatic memories
- **Brainspotting**: Works with specific eye positions to enable deep processing
- **Hypnotherapy**: Uses the hypnotic state for fast, direct change
- **SFBT (Solution Focused Brief Therapy)**: Focuses on solutions and future resources, not problems

### Important Note

As a psychotherapy practitioner, I am legally required: **I do not make healing promises.**

Instead, I can say: I have good experience with people who suffer from anxiety, panic attacks, depression, and trauma. Many of my clients report positive changes. I work with methods that have proven themselves — but every person is different.

The goal of my work: You should feel strong and confident and say: **"I can do something — and I will do it, because I know how."**
